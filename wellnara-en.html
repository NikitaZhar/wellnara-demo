Getting started is easier than you think

From the invitation
to the first appointment.

Four simple steps, and your appointment is on the calendar.

Set up and invite.

Add services with durations and prices, packages if you like, and your schedule — then invite your client by email.

Record the payment.

Your client pays in their usual way outside Wellnara; you add the amount to their wallet.

Your client chooses.

They choose the number of sessions and a time for the first appointment. The amount is reserved while the request awaits your decision.

You confirm.

The amount is charged, the package becomes active, and the first appointment is confirmed. Subsequent sessions are booked one at a time.

The decision is always yours. If a package request is declined or withdrawn before approval, the funds become available to the client again.

Good to know

The details that matter.

How does a client get access?

A practitioner registers by invitation from an administrator and invites clients by email. Each client has a dashboard with the practitioner's services, requests, appointments, and wallet.

Can clients pay by card within Wellnara?

Payment takes place outside the app; the practitioner records the amount received in the wallet. A request requires available funds or an unused session in a package. Balances are tracked in the practitioner's chosen currency.

How do session packages work?

The practitioner sets the maximum number of sessions; a package price, if configured, applies from two sessions onward. The full amount is reserved until approval. Then the package becomes active and the first appointment is confirmed. Remaining sessions are booked separately.

What happens when an appointment is rescheduled?

Clients can start rescheduling at least 12 hours in advance: the previous booking is canceled and the reservation released. A new time requires a new request and confirmation. The practitioner can cancel an appointment and, if needed, close the previous time slot.

What happens with cancellations or no-shows?

If canceled at least 24 hours in advance, the funds or session become available again. With a later cancellation or no-show, the reserved funds or session are used; if the practitioner cancels, they are released. These time limits are fixed in the current version.

What if the practitioner declines an appointment request?

The practitioner gives a reason, which the client sees in their dashboard, and the reservation is released. If the chosen time has not yet passed, that slot is closed and the client can choose another.

Are there reminders and calendar sync?

Emails include ICS files, and appointments can be added to Google Calendar, Outlook, and Apple Calendar. There are no automatic 24-hour reminders or two-way calendar sync; you can set a reminder in your own calendar.

How does a practitioner manage clients?

The practitioner invites clients by email and can see their list and individual wallets. A client can be temporarily marked inactive: they retain access to services and their history, but cannot book. The practitioner can reactivate them at any time.

How are security and passwords handled?

Registration is invitation-only, and links expire. Passwords can be changed in the profile or reset by email; sign-in is temporarily blocked after several failed attempts. Each person can choose Russian or English for the interface and emails.
