# Wellnara

**A platform for private practice: services, booking, and payment — in one calm space.**

Wellnara takes on the routine that leaves a specialist with no energy for what matters most. Bookings, schedules, payments, and remaining sessions live inside the app and update themselves — so the specialist is left with the one thing it was all for: being present with another person.

Alongside this description sits a visual landing page (`wellnara-v3.html`) — open it to see the two cabinets and the logic of a session at a single glance.

---

## Who it's for

Wellnara fits **any field that has a set of services, booking for them, payment for them, and their delivery.** It isn't an app "for psychologists" or "for nutritionists" — it's a tool for a way of working, and that way of working is the same across very different specialists:

- **nutrition and health** — consultations, ongoing guidance, meal programs;
- **psychology and coaching** — sessions, support, working through a request;
- **teaching and lessons** — classes, courses, one-on-one tutoring;
- **wellness and self-care** — practices, programs, regular meetings.

If you have a list of services, people book time with you, pay for it, and you deliver it — Wellnara is for you. The field can be anything.

---

## How it works

At its heart are **two connected cabinets: the specialist's and the client's.** A single booking is visible to both, so the awkward "did you pay?" and "what time did we agree on?" simply disappear.

The path from first contact to the first meeting takes four calm steps:

1. **Set up and invite.** The specialist creates services with durations and prices, optionally session packages and a schedule, and invites the client by email.
2. **Payment.** The client pays in the usual way outside Wellnara, and the specialist records the received amount in the client's wallet.
3. **The client chooses.** The client picks the number of sessions and the time of the first meeting. The amount is reserved, and the request awaits the specialist's decision.
4. **Confirmation.** The specialist confirms — the cost is deducted, the package is activated, the first meeting is set. Further sessions are booked one at a time.

The decision always stays with the specialist: a declined or withdrawn request returns the reserved funds to the client.

## What's inside

**The specialist's cabinet.** Your services, your clients, your rhythm. A flexible schedule with working hours, days off, and buffers before and after a session, so meetings never overlap. Client management: invitations by email, a wallet for each client, the option to gently close access during a pause and reopen it later.

**The client's cabinet.** The specialist's services, available time, their own meetings, and their balance — all in plain view. Booking takes a couple of minutes, with no calls and no back-and-forth about timing. The status of a meeting, remaining sessions, and the history of transactions are always at hand.

**Transparent money and packages.** A wallet with an available balance, reservations, and remaining sessions — in one clear transaction history that both sides can see. Booking from a package reserves a session, and a completed meeting is counted as used.

**Calendar and notifications.** Confirmed meetings are added to your usual calendar — Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple Calendar (ICS). Confirmation and cancellation emails reach both sides.

**Two languages.** The interface and emails are in Russian or English, chosen by each participant. Times are shown in your time zone.

**Access by invitation.** A specialist starts by invitation from an administrator and invites clients themselves. Invitation links are valid for a limited time, and passwords can be changed or recovered by email.

## Why it's easy to work with

A specialist's work is about trust and presence. Everything else — agreeing on times, doing math in your head, chasing payments — Wellnara moves into the background, where it belongs. The client, meanwhile, gets a calm and clear way to care for themselves: pick a convenient time, see their meetings, and plan ahead.

Less routine for the specialist. More clarity for the client. More room for the meeting itself.
